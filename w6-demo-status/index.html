How we got here

  1. Sep 29 - the plan: port aura's workflow idea into the prototype. Four build cards (types, the proposal tool, the deterministic executor, the approval wire), one world-side card (ops tools + healing).
  2. Oct 5-7 - all five built and reviewed; 15 offline tests pin the executor and approval legs; two live propose-only runs prove the model can drive the surface (accepted with a quality watch-note).
  3. Oct 13 - demo-prep plan written, frontier-reviewed over three rounds (PASS), Stage-0 rulings from Mike; assembly + canary green same night.
  4. Oct 14, tonight - seven live runs. The heal lands (run 1b: 3/3 steps applied, incident resolved). The unwind lands (run 2e: canary fails, both completed steps rolled back in reverse, nothing residual). Deny and hold-timeout legs exercised along the way. ~3.4M subscription tokens total.

What's left before the demo

  1. The handout (about half an hour): exact run commands with the canary as step zero, the two reference prompts, expected observations in order, failure signatures, revert steps. Then a quick drift check over it.
  2. A dry run with Mike driving (his ruling): one heal run where you type the command, watch the stream, and click the approve - plus a look at the render transcript, so the controls feel familiar before an audience.
  3. Housekeeping, on your word: four board commits sit unpushed (push approval is yours by convention). The W5 pull-request decision (the world-side branch) still sits with you - post-demo is fine.
  4. After the demo: W14 and W15 cards, the W5 PR, and closing W6's Gate T once you've watched both runs.
