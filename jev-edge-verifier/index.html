The JEV edge gate

An external explainer of the demo ahead: an observe-only verifier that scores every completed task edge in an agent DAG against the tool evidence the worker actually captured, and files one verdict artifact per edge.

The verdict rule, in decision order

OrderConditionVerdict
1evidence_presence < 0.30INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE - too little evidence shown to judge at all
2composite < 0.50, or any dimension below its 0.30 cutoff, or an information-loss truncation flagFLAG{dims} - names the failed dimensions
3otherwisePASS

Presence is the workhorse dimension: starvation alone separates the distractor, lossy-handoff, and fabricated classes nearly perfectly, so INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE is signal, not noise. Grounding was demoted to audit telemetry by its own pre-registered no-go rule: on clean-vs-fabricated pairs it drew ahead on 3 of 47 (rich arm) while presence did on 41 of 47, an 80.9-point gap. Per-claim grounding stats stay in the payload as the drift signal.

Tiering: the decision record

OptionShapeRuling
A - two-tier chosenCheap head-truncation screen on every submitted edge; a cheap non-PASS triggers a rich claim-indexed re-score; the rich verdict supersedes where triggered; the payload carries both tiers.Ports. The tier used is a required column in the verifier-on analysis, on every verdict, cheap-only included.
B - rich onlyEvery edge gets the claim-indexed assembly; no screen.Rejected: ships a two-tier claim the seam never exercises, and quietly turns the treatment arm of the verifier-on comparison into rich-only.
C - both, alwaysEvery edge judged both ways; paired verdicts.Rejected: doubles the machinery for paired data the comparison method does not consume. The pair is reproducible offline through the rescore path.

Truncation, three ways

ClassMeaningScored how
provenancethe cut happened upstream of the verifier, capture-siderecorded, never flagged
budgetingour bounded excerpt still covering its claims; the cut is visibly labeledrecorded, never flagged
lossa supported claim outside the shown text, or cut worker outputFLAG{truncation}

The distinction is earned, not aesthetic. When the research spike conflated the three, FLAG{truncation} degenerated into a size detector that fired on 19 of 20 honest edges. And a claim that loses its window in a starved rich budget keeps its found_in marker with a null window, so presence stays computable from markers alone.

Caveats and sources

What this page does not show: anything live. Every number comes from the offline corpus spike; no verdict has yet run on a real orchestration edge. The thresholds stay provisional until the re-check on labelled baseline edges. Version 1 is observe-only - no verdict crosses the wire, and worker scheduling is untouched while the verifier runs. No gating policy exists yet; that is by design.

Limits inherited from the research spike, stated at adoption

  • Adversarial-injection sensitivity is 57.7% at the shipped threshold - a threshold choice, not a separation failure (pooled AUROC on that class is 0.93 rich / 0.81 cheap).
  • Honest edges draw false accusations at 8.5% (rich) and 25.5% (cheap) at reject grade.
  • No independent blinded judge has audited the gate's agreement yet.
  • Claim extraction resolves about 66% of clean-edge claims not_found (worker-derived values, prose mentions), so audit telemetry inherits that floor.
  • Operational detail - token quotas, session estimates - is deliberately elided; the board is the source of truth.
